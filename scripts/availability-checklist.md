Current providers are copied from the illustrative, unverified catalogue; they are not confirmed availability. The JustWatch India links are search shortcuts, not verification. Leave catalogue changes for a separate review. Fill in Correct providers with offer types and Verified with the date checked (YYYY-MM-DD).

**Sort basis:** descending appearance count using a catalogue-wide audit profile with all 10 OTT apps and all eight languages selected, free-only off. Count one appearance per title in the “On your services” rail, each language rail and “Trending this week”, plus each of the five Home moods’ top four picks (Laugh, Edge-of-seat, Family, Feel-good, Mind-bender). Uses `homeSelections` and `dataSource.getMoodPicks`; excludes the hero carousel and the non-Home “Drama night” mood. This is a reproducible selection count, not measured traffic; personal preferences and free-only mode change the order. Ties sort by title, year, language, then ID.
